Meet Crossword Agent: a constraint-driven solver. The language model proposes ranked clue answers; Python owns the grid, crossing checks, and final validation. At first, the top guesses, CAR and AGE, look plausible. But crossing letters contradict them, so the agent prunes those candidates and propagates the new constraints. The grid resolves to CAT, ARE, and TEN across and down, and all six entries pass validation. On our bundled smoke test, independent guesses reach sixty-seven percent word accuracy; constraints and the full agent reach one hundred percent. That is one tiny puzzle, not a general performance claim. A separate adversarial unit test also proves the agent can backtrack after a confident wrong branch.
